# Mobile veterinary report and locality dossier — public site and PDFs

## Surface

- Scope: the published market report, the internal locality dossier, and their A4 PDF twins. One Astro project, two documents, one Markdown source each.
- Visitor mode: Read. The visitor arrives to understand a market and decide where to place a service.
- Audience: the veterinary founding team, the internal R&D team slicing the locality data, then investors and partners.
- Job: establish the launch verdict, the unit economics behind it, and which hundred localities the decision rests on.
- Action: agree the launch corridor, the first route, and the five conditions attached to it.
- Proof: 83 cited sources in the market report, 14 in the dossier, every figure carrying a provenance tier, every table generated from the dataset.
- Constraints: the documents are the product. The site and the PDFs render from one source and must never drift. Indian units and conventions throughout.

## Direction

- Chosen direction: The Sign Band, an international airport sign system.
- Memorable moment: the arrow. A single black arrow locked to the edge of the decision board points at the gate the reader should take next, and repaints the instant a filter changes.
- The verdict is a band, not a stamp: black on saturated yellow, one message, the key figure monumental beneath it.
- Sources sit in a stack of white panels, each with a black square in place of a pictogram, its id and publisher in mono.

## Unresolved

- The dossier route stays internal and noindex until the team says otherwise. It is a working surface, not a published one.
- The corpus's tier C household base for the south-east cluster is carried in the data and on every figure it touches. It is not resolved here.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A market assessment presented as a chain of decision points, the way an airport signs the way onward. It refuses the report-site default of a hero band over stacked white cards on a grey page, and it refuses the clinical-documentary default of a form with flagged result values. Every claim is a destination: where to go, how far, how long it takes, and what it costs.

OWN-WORLD: One saturated yellow, reserved for wayfinding and nothing else, and it has exactly eight uses: the masthead band, the section openers, the gate bands on the decision board, the target gate on the route map, the one hour the demand curve says to go, the one day of the week, the one month of the year, and the top row of the revenue ranking. In every case it marks the one thing the reader should go to. Everything else is black type on white, a warm paper ground behind the panels, and black hairline rules. Public Sans, a humanist sans, carries prose, band messages and the monumental gate numbers; Roboto Mono carries every parameter, numeral in a table, unit convention, ward code and source id. Yellow never fills a page and never becomes a background behind body text. There is no grain, no perforation, no film, no card corner, no drop shadow. Every panel edge is a drawn rule. A pictogram is a black square with a white mark, and no pictogram is decorative.

STORY: A partner knows within seconds that this is a go or no-go on a house-call veterinary practice in Chennai, that the answer is a launch in the south-east corridor on the Velachery route, and that it rests on a Rs 1,100 minimum blended price against a Rs 850 cost and a Rs 699 market. They follow the argument section by section, see every number with its provenance attached, and take the same documents away as PDFs that read identically.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed yellow masthead band carrying the report title in black at 64px on the left and a one-line deck beneath it, with the document's four parameters in mono on a white strip pinned to the band's lower edge. Below it the sign gantry: a sticky row of yellow section bands, the current one inverted black on yellow with an arrow beneath it, the rest yellow with black text. Then the reading sheet: white, ruled, black body text at a 68-character measure. The dossier carries a thirteen-rem margin column down the right holding the unit conventions, the provenance legend and how to read a gate; the market report carries its conventions inline, because its caveats belong in the sentences that hold them. The verdict band sits at the top of the sheet, black on yellow, one message, with the Rs 1,100 key figure monumental beneath it on a white panel whose scale is painted from the floor and target values themselves and marked with the prices Chennai operators actually publish. Below the masthead a yellow band leads to the other document.

FORM: The Sign Band, an international airport sign system, presented first in the bolder register of the direction round, key 2d842ca6, re-rolled twice and dealt first in the final hand. Code-led: no comp was owed, and the ambition lives in this contract.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
