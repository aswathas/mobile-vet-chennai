# 003 — Replace the visual world with a sign system

**Date.** 2026-10-24
**Supersedes.** Beat 3's implicit world, the cutting bench. The world that
shipped in `attempt 003` is retired.
**Change.** The whole site was rebuilt in a new world: an international airport
sign system. One saturated yellow, reserved for wayfinding and nothing else.
Public Sans for prose and monumental numbers, Roboto Mono for every parameter.
Black hairline rules, no grain, no film, no cards, no shadows, no second
accent. The masthead is an overhead band, the navigation is a gantry of section
bands with the current one inverted, the verdict is a band rather than a
stamp, and the sources are a stack of panels with black square marks.

The dossier gained the dossier's own instruments: a chain diagram from
households to the revenue one route can reach, a diagram of the two corridors
as lines of gates, a decision board with live filters and an arrow that
repaints, two corridor buttons that open each side directly, and a margin
column carrying units, the provenance legend and how to read a gate.

**Why.** The corpus outgrew the incumbent frame, and the user asked for a data
showcase rather than a longer version of the same document. Three rounds of
direction exploration were run; the user rerolled twice, the last time with the
steer "bolder", and the sign band led its own hand.

**What it cost.** The market report's own typographic character changed
completely, and its PDF is a different document from the one that shipped. The
content did not change by a word.
