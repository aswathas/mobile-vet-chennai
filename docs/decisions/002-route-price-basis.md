# 002 — Price measured on the route

**Date.** 2026-10-24
**Supersedes.** The locality-level blended price in the two research passes.
**Change.** Every locality in the dossier carries the blended price of the route
it rides: the demand-weighted mean of the localities' own our-mix demand-pool
averages on the same route cluster. The locality's own fill-diluted number is
kept beside it as `locality_fill_blended_price` and is no longer what the
verdict reads.

**Why.** A locality that cannot fill a route on its own gets its price diluted
with off-peak consultations, so a locality-level price measures the size of the
locality rather than the market. Under the old rule Zone A was full of
no-go verdicts at a blended price around Rs 770, which said more about how the
clusters had been cut than about the market's willingness to pay.

The market report's own economics are route-level — four billable services per
trip, six visits a day, about 156 visits a month — so the price that decides a
route has to be measured at the route. Cluster blends now run Rs 1,295 to
Rs 1,311 in the south-east corridor and Rs 1,384 to Rs 1,557 in the OMR
corridor, and both clear the Rs 1,100 floor. The floor stops separating the
corridors; the competition and the demand do.
