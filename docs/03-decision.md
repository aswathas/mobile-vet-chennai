# The decisions

## Launch in Zone A, on the Velachery cluster

Zone A holds 11,576 of the 16,583 cases a month the two corridors hold, and
Rs 1.51 crore of the Rs 2.24 crore the practice mix could reach. Its mean
competition saturation is 31.8 against Zone B's 59.6, its weekend lift is the
strongest in the corpus, and its season is the flattest.

What ruled out the alternatives:

**Zone B, the OMR and ECR corridor.** It has the better price band — cluster
blends of Rs 1,384 to Rs 1,557 against Rs 1,295 to Rs 1,311 — and the best
single route in the corpus, Rs 91,728 of contribution against the Velachery
route's Rs 70,668. It was ruled out of first place on two grounds, neither of
them price. Thirteen of its fifty localities already fail on saturation, eight
of them because the Little Angels home-visit arm is physically based in
Navalur, so a quarter of the corridor is contested ground before the first
visit. And its demand is thinner: 5,007 cases a month against Zone A's 11,576.
It is the second route, not the first.

**A route that runs on both corridors.** Rejected because the OMR spine and
the south-east cluster are thirty kilometres apart with the Adyar and Guindy
corridor between them. A route that serves both spends its day in traffic and
its contribution on fuel.

**Velachery against Medavakkam Town as the first route.** Velachery main wins
on volume, 838 cases a month against 721, and it carries the highest peak cover
in the corpus at 51 cases in a day, which matters for a route that wants to
fill a Saturday. Medavakkam Town has the lighter competition, saturation 36
against 55, and the airport corridor's new households on its western edge. The
two are worth about Rs 4,000 a route a month in contribution terms, which is
noise next to the difference in how hard each one is to defend. Velachery
first, Medavakkam second.

## Measure demand against the city, not against each corridor

The two research passes did not agree, and the disagreement was an artefact. One
measured veterinary demand per household a year, the other per pet-owning
household a year, so the OMR corridor looked six times larger than the
south-east cluster. Set side by side they were not two views of one market;
they were two different units.

Everything in the dossier is now measured on one city-level calibration: the
Greater Chennai Corporation's own working figures from the August to October
2026 vaccination campaign, 1,50,303 dogs vaccinated against a 2,00,000 target,
spread over the 1,733,423 households the 2011 Census and the Corporation's
property-tax growth give, with veterinary demand at 4.0 contacts per
pet-owning household a year.

Recorded in `decisions/001` and in `attempts/001`.

## Measure price on the route, not on the locality

A locality that cannot fill a route on its own gets a price diluted with
off-peak consultations, so a locality-level price measures the size of the
locality rather than the market. Every locality in the dossier now carries the
blended price of the route it rides, and the locality's own fill-diluted number
is kept beside it.

Recorded in `decisions/002`.

## Replace the visual world rather than extend it

The cutting-bench world was good and it was already shipped. It was replaced
because the corpus outgrew it: ten tables and eighty-three sources fit inside
punched windows, a hundred localities and six hundred competitor rows do not.

The world that replaced it is an airport sign system. It was chosen over
eleven other candidates, three rounds of which the user rerolled — once for a
cleaner hand, once because the first hand did not commit, and once with the
steer "bolder", which is how the sign band ended up leading its own hand.

## What would reopen these decisions

**The price floor.** Every GO locality clears Rs 1,100 because that is the
condition attached. A corridor whose actual blended price lands under it stops
being a GO, whatever its demand column says. Zone A's market price of Rs 951 is
the number that should worry the founding team.

**The first three months of bookings.** The dossier models demand, not
willingness to pay. If owners will not pay Rs 1,199 for an extended
consultation with a written plan, the mix has to change, not the route.

**The cluster definition.** A route needs four billable services per trip to
cost Rs 850. If the bookings do not fill a cluster's stops, the cost per visit
rises and the fix is fewer localities, not more bookings.

**A partner clinic.** Route-only economics have no facility to absorb a slow
month, a heat wave or a week when the veterinarian is ill. A signed partner
clinic changes which clusters are worth opening first.
