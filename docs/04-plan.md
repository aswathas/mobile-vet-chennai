# The plan as executed

This is a record of what was done and in what order. It is not a procedure.
Where the order changed, the change is in `decisions/`.

## Phase 1 — The corpus

Two research passes, one per corridor, run in parallel by external agents
against a written schema. Each pass collected its own census, property-tax,
street-inventory, operator-price and mapping evidence, and each wrote four
files: the dataset, a source list, a model note and a narrative analysis.

Zone A came back with a sound household model — a modelled built-footprint
density reconciled against the Corporation's density — and fifty localities.
Zone B came back with a census and property-tax allocation and fifty
localities, and a household model that was three orders of magnitude out
because it never used the ward totals it had computed.

## Phase 2 — The audit and the repair

A schema validator and an arithmetic auditor went in before anything was
rendered. They found the Zone B household defect, a verdict rule that had been
tuned against the broken scale, and two passes that had never written their
source lists or model notes. Both passes were repaired, in place, by agents
given the specific defect rather than a request to try again.

## Phase 3 — The calibration

Both zones were put onto one city-level basis, and the three corrections were
written into the report itself rather than into a build script: the unit
mismatch, the price-basis mismatch, and a tier mark that had overstated
half the corpus.

## Phase 4 — The report

The dossier prose was written by hand, with every table and every figure
generated from the dataset so that nothing can drift between the argument and
the evidence. A truth audit was written alongside it, holding forty-seven
claims the prose makes and checking each one against the data.

## Phase 5 — The site

The market report kept its content and changed its world. The dossier was built
as a second route on the same site with its own source, its own PDF and its
own decision board. Both were rebuilt in the sign band, then put through a
design finish review, twice, with the findings fixed in batches.

## Phase 6 — The record

This chronicle, the design record, and the repository itself.
